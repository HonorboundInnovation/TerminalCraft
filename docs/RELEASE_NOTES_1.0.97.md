# TerminalCraft 1.0.97 — sensor and network-device models

All eleven standalone sensor variants now share a neutral housing with their
existing identifying artwork mapped to the exposed display face. Removed
decorative protrusions outside their selection geometry. The sensor array
keeps its geometry with clearer casing and panel separation.

The router has one solid front panel instead of overlapping port overlays.
The wireless display link retains its antenna shape with distinct panels.
Both devices now have selection/collision shapes matching their models.

No texture files, sensing behavior, networking rules, IDs, protocol or saved
formats change in this slice. Includes the preceding cabinet and storage model
work. This is a development build; full catalog approval and remaining feature,
integration, multiplayer, recovery and performance qualification remain open.
